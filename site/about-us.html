About Cooldatasoft

Engineers who take
data seriously.

Cooldatasoft is a software engineering and consultancy company focused on data — building the platforms, pipelines and software that make information useful.

Who we are

Cooldatasoft is a software engineering and consultancy company with a single focus: helping organisations get real value out of their data. We build custom software, design and operate data platforms and pipelines, and advise teams on the architecture and tooling that will actually serve them.

We're engineers first. That means we care about the unglamorous parts — data quality, tests, monitoring and maintainability — because those are what separate a demo from a system you can rely on.

Our focus

Data platforms

Reliable ingestion, transformation and warehousing, built to scale with you.

Custom software

Applications, tools and APIs built around your data and your workflows.

Consultancy

Straight advice on architecture, tooling and the data roadmap.

What we value

Correctness

If the numbers can't be trusted, nothing built on them can. We engineer for accuracy.

Pragmatism

The simplest thing that solves the problem, not the trendiest thing on the market.

Partnership

We leave your team more capable than we found it, not more dependent on us.
