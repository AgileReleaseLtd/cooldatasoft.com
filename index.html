What we do

Engineering and consultancy across the whole data lifecycle — from raw source to decision.

Data platforms & pipelines

We design and build the plumbing that moves your data reliably — ingestion, transformation, warehousing and orchestration. Batch or streaming, on the cloud of your choice, with tests and monitoring so you find out about a broken pipeline before your stakeholders do.

Technical consultancy

Data architecture reviews, tooling decisions and a pragmatic roadmap. We help you choose the right stack — and avoid the expensive one you don't need.

Custom software

Web apps, internal tools and APIs built around your data — designed, delivered and maintained by a team that sweats the details.

Analytics & BI

Metrics, dashboards and reporting people actually use — with definitions everyone agrees on and numbers they can trust.

Cloud data infrastructure

Scalable, cost-aware infrastructure with the observability and governance to run it safely in production.
